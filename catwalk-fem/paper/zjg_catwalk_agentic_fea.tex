\begin{abstract}
This paper records a complete, auditable agentic finite-element modelling run
for the Zhangjinggao construction catwalk. A 77\,MB millimetre centre-line STEP
is mapped to \(x=\mathrm{chainage}-K16+876.000\). Floor-rope and portal-rope
anchors are disjoint \texttt{*NSET}/\texttt{*BOUNDARY} families. Twenty-one
cross-passages and 142 portal frames (71 stations per deck; Chinese classifier
U+6980, not U+698C) reconcile with zero insertions.

Frozen deck
\texttt{82548e6a3bd2612b6b39a08c313402b32a1961af6eba018158267906276ab6da}
keeps its illegal ELSET+uniaxial \texttt{TYPE=STRESS} card and is not rewritten.
Site
\texttt{41fb32225489b0c6f993d3a077ce9293d472e4ede5ff644ca170bebbbbca924a}
is the IC-pass / first-increment-singular deck and is not rewritten. An
independent reread of that site recovers 22\,096 used-node components and
19\,371 two-node fragments \(=\) 17\,756 T3D2 \(+\) 1\,615 B31. The integer
21\,426 counts \textbf{components that contain no constrained node}, not
unconstrained nodes (50\,676). The three-card boundary union is 1\,220.

The new main deck
\texttt{c635dad78661e6495bdf829b0c97f8610b5fedb8603307e806860032a70cda84}
attaches drawing-anchor stubs at \(x=-23.895/-44.909/4225.7\), consumes T3D2
yarn gaps and B31 pairs, pins leftover unconstrained \emph{components}, and
writes 421\,432 eight-field second Piola--Kirchhoff rows. CalculiX 2.21 reads
it, assembles 1\,401\,126 equations, factors the tangent (not singular), and
fails to converge the first increment (exit 201, 63.65\,s). The \texttt{.frd}
file is an 80-byte title shell with no DISP. No spectrum is claimed.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}
\label{sec:intro}

\paragraph{Related work.}
Automated CAD-to-FEM pipelines usually inherit one coordinate system and one
support family. A multi-span suspension-bridge construction catwalk does not:
two walkways, two rope families with different physical anchor stations,
discrete portal frames, and discrete cross-passages, all written in highway
chainage. Theory archive \texttt{catwalk-theory/thirteen-mode-models} v1.2
freezes drawing topology and the fourteen-mode isolation protocol; it is not
a CalculiX deck. Pull request \#18 shipped an empty \texttt{artifacts/}.
Pull request \#19 first deposited \texttt{82548e6a}, then \texttt{41fb3222}.
This run does not rewrite those two sites. It emits a third hash.

\paragraph{Method.}
The run follows \texttt{catwalk-fem/SKILL.md}. Geometry is STEP-only.
Materials, sag, and loads come from drawings and the check report. The
coordinate gate is identity unless saddle-height evidence requires a shift,
never \(X-x_{\min}\). Topology is reconciled against frozen drawing lists:
21 passages and \(71\times 2=142\) portal frames. Anchors are two families.
\texttt{82548e6a} and \texttt{41fb3222} are frozen. The new main deck is
written to \texttt{zjg\_catwalk\_main.inp}. CalculiX runs only on a copy.
A successful read, a factorable tangent, or a \texttt{.sta} increment row is
not a solved statics claim. Solved means exit 0, DISP in \texttt{.frd}, and
a converged increment row.

\paragraph{Experiment.}
The centre-line STEP (139\,991 trimmed curves,
\texttt{SI\_UNIT(.MILLI.,.METRE.)}) is converted to metres. After identity
mapping, STEP nodes satisfy \(X\in[0,4270.609]\)\,m and
\(Z_{\max}=350.312\)\,m. Three SHA-256 values were rechecked on this host.
CalculiX 2.21 on a copy of \texttt{c635dad7} assembled 1\,401\,126 equations
and failed to converge the first increment.

\paragraph{Outlook.}
The new hash is emitted. The first increment is no longer immediately
singular because of 22\,096 components. Static convergence still requires
work on the remaining 1\,073 components, 477 pair fragments, four
unconstrained components, and the \texttt{umpc\_mean\_rot} failure---not a
rewrite of the two frozen sites. TARGET-FREQ stays isolated until a solved
spectrum exists.

\section{Related work and isolation protocol}
\label{sec:iso}

\paragraph{Related work.}
Agentic FEA here means a scripted chain with frozen constants. CalculiX 2.21
is used because the keyword deck is inspectable. Abaqus-like ELSET-wide
uniaxial prestress is not the CalculiX 2.21 contract.

\paragraph{Method.}
Isolation rules from theory v1.2 \S1 and the skill file:
\begin{enumerate}
\item STEP supplies coordinates only.
\item Properties come from DRW-A/B, CALC-INPUT, STD, or a named ASSUMP.
\item \texttt{TARGET-FREQ} lives in \texttt{catwalk-fem/isolated/} and is not
imported by \texttt{write\_inp} or the solver deck.
\item Formed sag is \(h=227.300\)\,m. The \(255.56\)\,m main-cable control sag
is forbidden in the deck (verified absent).
\item The two walkways keep independent degrees of freedom.
\item Floor-rope anchors and portal-rope anchors are never unioned.
\item After SHA-256 \texttt{82548e6a} and \texttt{41fb3222} are frozen, those
\texttt{.inp} files are not rewritten to ``make the solver happy''.
\end{enumerate}

\paragraph{Experiment.}
The isolated file lists fourteen frequencies starting at \(0.0296\)\,Hz.
Full-text search of all three decks finds neither \(0.0296\) nor \(255.56\).
\texttt{write\_inp.py} does not import \texttt{isolated/TARGET-FREQ.json}.

\paragraph{Outlook.}
Isolation is a scientific claim. Later comparison tables may load TARGET-FREQ
only after deck, solve, and mode-shape classification are frozen.

\section{Ingestion and classification (N02--N07)}

\paragraph{Related work.}

\paragraph{Method.}

metres. \texttt{classify\_segments} labels floor ropes by the drawing
\(y=\pm(21.45\pm(0.85+0.26k))\) lattice, long high members as portal/handrail

inter-deck members as \texttt{cross\_passage}.

dropped from the coarsened deck.

\paragraph{Outlook.}

is reported in \S\ref{sec:disc} and is not hidden by the 142 frame count.

\section{Materials, family anchors, and initial tension (N08--N10)}
\label{sec:mat}

\paragraph{Related work.}
Theory v1.2 \S4 forbids treating floor-rope anchors as portal-rope anchors.
The two north stations differ by 21\,m; the two south stations differ by
15.332\,m.

\paragraph{Method.}
Wire rope \(E=1.20\times10^{11}\)\,Pa, floor/portal
\(A=1400.42\times10^{-6}\)\,m\(^2\), \(\mu=12.038\)\,kg/m. Steel
\(E=2.06\times10^{11}\)\,Pa, \(\rho=7850\)\,kg/m\(^3\). Horizontal force
\[
H=\frac{wL^2}{8h},\quad h=227.300~\mathrm{m},\quad L=2286.642~\mathrm{m}
\]
gives \(H_{\mathrm{floor}}=7.954\)\,MN and \(H_{\mathrm{portal}}=2.039\)\,MN,
hence \(\sigma_{\mathrm{floor}}=3.549611\times10^{8}\)\,Pa and
\(\sigma_{\mathrm{portal}}=2.426295\times10^{8}\)\,Pa. Floor and portal
anchors use two \texttt{*BOUNDARY} cards. A third card pins saddles and
nominal ends. The new main adds a fourth card for leftover unconstrained
\emph{components}; that set is not unioned into the anchors.

\paragraph{Experiment.}
On \texttt{41fb3222}, north floor and north portal remain STEP-end proxies
(\(x=0\) and \(x\approx 46.4\)). On \texttt{c635dad7}, drawing stubs place
FLOOR\_N at \(-23.895\), PORTAL\_N at \(-44.909\), and PORTAL\_S at
\(4225.7\). South floor remains a match (\(\Delta=0.38\)\,m). In both decks
\(N_{\mathrm{floor}}=312\), \(N_{\mathrm{portal}}=16\), intersection empty.

\paragraph{Outlook.}
Stubs write drawing \(x<0\) into the new main only. They do not rewrite
\texttt{41fb3222}.
