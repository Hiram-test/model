\begin{abstract}
A millimetre STEP of the Zhangjinggao catwalk centre-lines is mapped to
\(x=\mathrm{chainage}-K16+876.000\), classified against drawing topology,
and written as a complete CalculiX deck. Floor-rope and portal-rope anchors
are disjoint. Twenty-one passages and 142 portal frames reconcile with zero
drawing insertions. Main-deck SHA-256 is
\texttt{82548e6a3bd2612b6b39a08c313402b32a1961af6eba018158267906276ab6da}.
Twenty-six gates PASS. \texttt{ccx} was not run.
\end{abstract}
